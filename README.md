# senior_research
Research project on the ethics of cybersecurity focusing on gray-hat hacking.

# Ethical Bounds of Gray-Hat Hacking

## Research Objective
This research will investigate where poeple draw the ethical line between unacceptable and acceptable hacking.
This study will examine whether people's opinions of an organization influence how ethically they determine hacking activities.

## Methodology
Two anonymous surveys will be conducted with the same ethical scenarios
Survey 1 will be based on oranizations that are disliked
Survey 2 will be based on organizations that are preferred
Participants will rate how ethical they consider the scenarios and explain.

## Expected Outcomes
This study will determine whether personal opionions about an organization will influece ethical decisions of gray-hat hacking.
